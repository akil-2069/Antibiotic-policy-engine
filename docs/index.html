Antibiotic Policy Engine

Choose an ICU and a syndrome. The engine takes the regimens ICMR allows, checks each one against that ICU's own resistance data, applies the hospital's restrictions, and either recommends a regimen or tells you to call Infectious Disease.

Patient factors

How a recommendation is made

      
    
        
  1. Guideline. Take every regimen ICMR lists for the syndrome.
    2. 
        
  2. Specimen. Map the syndrome to its culture type, e.g. VAP to respiratory aspirates.
    3. 
        
  3. Local data. Keep the first isolate per patient, then measure how many isolates each regimen fails to cover.
    4. 
        
  4. Policy. Remove contraindicated or non-formulary drugs and flag ID-approval drugs.
    5. 
        
  5. Decide. Recommend the best safe or caution regimen, or escalate if every option is above 30%.
